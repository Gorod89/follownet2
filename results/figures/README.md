# Figures of the report

Written by `scripts/make_report.py` (docs/leaderboard.md, section 4; style in `trafficloop/figures/style.py`): 160 mm wide, 9 pt text, PNG at 200 dpi and PDF with the same content. Families: network blue, mechanistic vermillion, baseline bluish green (Okabe-Ito). One line per file:

- `e1_persistence_ratio_bars.png` (PNG, 200 dpi): E1 (H5.1): P0 acceleration RMSE relative to persistence, NGSIM and FollowNet highD, driver split, fold 0, seed 0; bars from 1 on a log axis, 95 % driver-bootstrap intervals, models sorted by the ratio, persistence is the line at 1 (`results/m4/e1_ratios.csv`).
- `e1_persistence_ratio_bars.pdf` (PDF, vector): E1 (H5.1): P0 acceleration RMSE relative to persistence, NGSIM and FollowNet highD, driver split, fold 0, seed 0; bars from 1 on a log axis, 95 % driver-bootstrap intervals, models sorted by the ratio, persistence is the line at 1 (`results/m4/e1_ratios.csv`).
- `e2_split_inflation_bars.png` (PNG, 200 dpi): E2 (H5.2): paired inflation (clean - leaky) / clean in % on the affected probe events of folds 0-4, NGSIM and OpenACC, P0 acceleration, P1 10 s and P1 whole-event spacing RMSE; whiskers: paired driver-bootstrap interval of clean - leaky divided by the clean error (`results/m4/e2_paired.csv`).
- `e2_split_inflation_bars.pdf` (PDF, vector): E2 (H5.2): paired inflation (clean - leaky) / clean in % on the affected probe events of folds 0-4, NGSIM and OpenACC, P0 acceleration, P1 10 s and P1 whole-event spacing RMSE; whiskers: paired driver-bootstrap interval of clean - leaky divided by the clean error (`results/m4/e2_paired.csv`).
- `e3_rank_bump_chart.png` (PNG, 200 dpi): E3 (H5.3): rank of every model under P0, P1 10 s, P1 whole event and the composite, NGSIM and FollowNet highD; rank 1 = lowest value, ties broken by the model name (`results/m4/e3_rankings.csv`).
- `e3_rank_bump_chart.pdf` (PDF, vector): E3 (H5.3): rank of every model under P0, P1 10 s, P1 whole event and the composite, NGSIM and FollowNet highD; rank 1 = lowest value, ties broken by the model name (`results/m4/e3_rankings.csv`).
- `e3_pareto_scatter.png` (PNG, 200 dpi): E3b: realism against the traffic-level composite: whole-event P1 spacing RMSE (log axis) against composite v2 (mean normalised rank of the concave-growth and hysteresis errors, the unstable fraction, the 100-vehicle collisions, the whole-event P1 spacing RMSE and the relative fundamental-diagram error; 0 = best) per model, NGSIM and FollowNet highD, family colours, every model labelled, the Pareto front of the two (both lower is better) as a step line, models on it with a dark rim (`results/m4/e3_pareto.csv`).
- `e3_pareto_scatter.pdf` (PDF, vector): E3b: realism against the traffic-level composite: whole-event P1 spacing RMSE (log axis) against composite v2 (mean normalised rank of the concave-growth and hysteresis errors, the unstable fraction, the 100-vehicle collisions, the whole-event P1 spacing RMSE and the relative fundamental-diagram error; 0 = best) per model, NGSIM and FollowNet highD, family colours, every model labelled, the Pareto front of the two (both lower is better) as a step line, models on it with a dark rim (`results/m4/e3_pareto.csv`).
- `e3_tau_summary.png` (PNG, 200 dpi): E3 across datasets: Kendall's tau between the one-step, the whole-event closed-loop and the composite rankings of the 24 entries, one group of bars per dataset with 95 % bootstrap intervals (`results/m4/e3_kendall.csv`).
- `e3_tau_summary.pdf` (PDF, vector): E3 across datasets: Kendall's tau between the one-step, the whole-event closed-loop and the composite rankings of the 24 entries, one group of bars per dataset with 95 % bootstrap intervals (`results/m4/e3_kendall.csv`).
- `e4_transfer_heatmap.png` (PNG, 200 dpi): E4 (P5): whole-event relative degradation rmse_s(A -> B) / rmse_s(B -> B) of 8 SUBSET models over 6 datasets (rows trained on, columns tested on), diverging log colour scale centred at 1 and capped at 0.1 and 10, values printed (`results/m4/e4_transfer.csv`).
- `e4_transfer_heatmap.pdf` (PDF, vector): E4 (P5): whole-event relative degradation rmse_s(A -> B) / rmse_s(B -> B) of 8 SUBSET models over 6 datasets (rows trained on, columns tested on), diverging log colour scale centred at 1 and capped at 0.1 and 10, values printed (`results/m4/e4_transfer.csv`).
